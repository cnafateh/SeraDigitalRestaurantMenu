# Image generation notes

The six local menu images were created as original, project-specific assets with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. Each final image was resized to 1280 × 853 and exported as WebP.

The shared direction across the set was: world-class contemporary restaurant photography, dark charcoal stone backdrop, soft directional daylight, rich natural contrast, realistic food texture, landscape 3:2 framing with safe card crops, and no people, hands, cutlery, text, logos or watermarks.

## Margherita

Editorial, photorealistic overhead three-quarter close-up of a freshly baked Neapolitan Margherita pizza with blistered leopard-spotted crust, San Marzano tomato, creamy fresh mozzarella, vivid basil and extra virgin olive oil on a dark charcoal stone tabletop. Natural and appetizing; no CGI look or excessive garnish.

## Diavola

Editorial photorealistic food photograph of a whole Neapolitan Diavola pizza with spicy salami, Calabrian chili, San Marzano tomato and melted fior di latte.

## Truffle Tagliatelle

Editorial photorealistic photograph of silky truffle tagliatelle, hand-cut ribbons with light Parmesan and delicate shaved black truffle, plated in a low matte charcoal bowl.

## Mediterranean Sea Bass

Editorial photorealistic photograph of a roasted Mediterranean sea bass fillet with crisp golden skin, spring peas, restrained lemon beurre blanc and fresh herbs on a dark ceramic plate.

## Burrata & Heirlooms

Editorial photorealistic photograph of fresh burrata with jewel-toned heirloom tomatoes, basil leaves, extra virgin olive oil and cracked black pepper on an off-white ceramic plate.

## Classic Tiramisu

Editorial photorealistic photograph of an elegant rectangular tiramisu portion with distinct mascarpone and espresso-soaked layers and a fine cocoa dusting on a dark ceramic plate.
